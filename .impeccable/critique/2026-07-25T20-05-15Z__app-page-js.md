## Design Specificity Verdict

The landing is strongly authored for Routecycle. Warm paper, dark technical
surfaces, route traces, mono telemetry, map-risk markers, confidence, and
surface/access controls form a coherent product-specific world. The weakness is
not generic styling but diluted persuasion: the production planner, internal
prototype, interest handoff, blog, admin, and research evidence appear as
competing product layers.

The deterministic detector returned `[]` with zero findings for `app/page.js`.

## Overall Impression

The route demonstration is more convincing than a conventional marketing hero,
and the visual system feels credible and specific. The largest opportunity is
to make the product boundary and next action as clear as the design itself.

## What Is Working

1. The map, risk markers, checks, confidence, alternatives, and metrics
   demonstrate explainability instead of merely claiming it.
2. Manrope, IBM Plex Mono, paper/dark contrast, square rules, and restrained

3. The implementation already includes visible labels, focus treatment,

## Priority Issues

### P1: Competing calls to action

The external planner, internal “test front,” method link, header interest link,
interactive demo, and feedback form ask visitors to resolve the product
topology before acting. Keep one primary action per stage, replace internal
terminology with rider language, and align the header interest action with the
feedback section.

Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`

### P1: Confidence and trust lack adjacent qualification

“Routes you can trust,” “without surprises,” and 92/100 can be read as safety
or access assurance even though the product explicitly rejects such
guarantees. Define confidence as completeness of available OSM signals and
place the limitation next to the score and route preview.

Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`

### P1: Email handoff overstates success

The interface sets success before a mail client can be verified and silently
ignores clipboard failure. Use “draft prepared,” report clipboard confirmation
separately, expose a copy fallback, retain data, and explain what to do if no
mail app opens.

Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

### P2: Explanation follows the commitment request

The method appears after the feedback form. Move a concise reasoning statement
closer to confidence and place enough method context before the interest ask.

Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`

### P2: Mobile interruption support

The mobile path is long, the form lacks autocomplete, and partial input is not
preserved. Reduce repeated actions, add autocomplete, and keep the route result
closer to its controls.

Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`
